    04 — How it works

    1. 01

      We measure the fingerprint AI models take from your face or voice.

    2. 02

      We nudge your file, invisibly, until that fingerprint points somewhere else.

    3. 03

      Clone and swap tools built on those models get the wrong person.

    Limits, plainly

    Shroud is tested against the specific open models it runs here: ArcFace and FaceNet for faces, ECAPA for voices. Tools built on them are affected most; others, including commercial services, may be affected less or not at all. Heavy compression, denoising or deliberate clean-up can wear the layer down. It makes cloning you more expensive. It does not make it impossible.
